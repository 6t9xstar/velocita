# Code of Conduct

Velocita follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through GitHub (@6t9xstar). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content, or temporarily or permanently ban contributors, for behaviour they judge harmful.
