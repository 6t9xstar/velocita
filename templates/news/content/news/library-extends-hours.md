title: Central library extends weekend opening hours
description: From November the central library opens until 9 pm on Saturdays and Sundays, after a pilot doubled evening visits.

tags: [city, culture]

The central library will stay open until 9 pm on weekends from November, after a three-month pilot.

Evening visits doubled during the pilot, with students making up most of the new visitors.
