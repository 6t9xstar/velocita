The city council on Thursday approved a 40-kilometre network of protected bike lanes, the largest cycling investment in its history.

## What was approved

The plan connects every district to the centre with physically separated lanes. Construction starts next spring.

## What happens next

Residents can comment on the detailed street designs until the end of the year. Read our [analysis of the costs](/news/what-the-bike-network-will-cost/).
