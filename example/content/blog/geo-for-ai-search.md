## llms.txt

Velocita generates `/llms.txt` and `/llms-full.txt` automatically.

## Markdown mirrors

Every page is also published as clean `.md` — e.g. this page lives at `/blog/geo-for-ai-search.md`.

## Structured data

`BlogPosting`, `BreadcrumbList`, `WebSite` and `FAQPage` JSON-LD are emitted per page.
